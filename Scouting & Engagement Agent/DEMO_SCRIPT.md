# Demo Script

Target length: 3 to 5 minutes

## 1. Problem framing

Show the landing screen and explain that recruiters often lose time in three places:

- converting JDs into searchable hiring signals
- identifying who actually matches
- figuring out who is likely to respond with real interest

## 2. Input the job description

- click `Load Sample Job`
- point out the role title, experience bar, and technical stack
- click `Find and Engage Candidates`

## 3. Walk through the pipeline

As the progress panel updates, narrate:

- the JD parser extracts skills and responsibilities
- the matcher compares those signals with the mock candidate pool
- the engagement engine simulates outreach and candidate interest
- the ranking layer combines fit and intent into one recruiter-ready shortlist

## 4. Review the shortlist

Highlight:

- the top ranked candidate
- match score vs interest score
- explanation boxes and recruiter recommendations
- exported JSON

## 5. Close

Summarize that the prototype gives recruiters an immediate shortlist instead of a raw pile of profiles.
